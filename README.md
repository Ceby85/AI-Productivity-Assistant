# Brew & Bloom AI

Here’s a cleaner, more focused prompt specifically aligned with your CAPACITI project brief. The brief requires a real-world business use case, strong prompt engineering, at least three AI features, responsible AI, and a demonstrable productivity improvement.

Act as a business strategist, coffee-shop consultant, and AI productivity specialist.

I want to create a business outline for a modern coffee shop in South Africa that will also be used as an AI-powered workplace productivity project.

Develop a realistic and practical coffee-shop concept that demonstrates how AI can reduce repetitive work, improve efficiency, support decision-making, and improve customer service.

7. AI-Powered Productivity Assistant

Design an AI assistant specifically for the coffee shop.

The solution must include at least three AI features, in line with the project requirements. The available project examples include an AI task planner, research assistant, smart email generator, meeting-note summarizer, and chatbot interface.

For the coffee shop, prioritise these features:

Feature 1: AI Daily Task Planner

Creates daily and weekly staff/manager task lists

Prioritises urgent tasks

Suggests efficient schedules

Identifies missed or overdue tasks

Feature 2: AI Inventory Assistant

Reviews inventory information

Identifies items that may need reordering

Creates supplier order lists

Helps reduce waste

Provides inventory recommendations

Feature 3: AI Marketing Assistant

Creates social media posts

Generates promotional ideas

Creates weekly marketing calendars

Writes customer-facing promotional messages

Adapts content for different audiences

You may also suggest additional features such as:

AI Customer Feedback Summarizer

AI Sales Insights Assistant

AI Staff Schedule Assistant

AI Customer Service Chatbot

For every AI feature, provide:

Business problem

AI solution

User input

Example prompt

Example AI response

Productivity benefit

How the result would be validated by a human

8. Prompt Engineering

Create at least five high-quality prompts for the coffee-shop AI assistant.

Each prompt must contain:

Role

Context

Task

Specific instructions

Constraints

Desired output format

Make the prompts detailed enough to demonstrate strong prompt-engineering skills.

Explain briefly why each prompt is effective and how it could be improved after testing.

9. Responsible AI

Identify risks including:

Incorrect AI recommendations

Customer privacy

Bias

Incorrect inventory or financial recommendations

Over-reliance on AI

For each risk, provide a practical safeguard.

Include a rule that important business decisions must be reviewed and approved by a human before action is taken. The project specifically requires consideration of AI limitations, bias, risks, disclaimers, and validation.

Day 5 — Finalisation & Presentation

Test the complete solution

Prepare the presentation

This follows the structure and expected outputs in the supplied project brief.

12. Final Output

Present the completed business outline in a professional, concise format suitable for a CAPACITI AI project presentation.

End with:

Business problem

Proposed solution

Target users

Three or more AI features

The project should clearly demonstrate problem relevance, prompt-engineering quality, functionality, innovation, responsible AI, and presentation readiness, matching the evaluation criteria in the project brief.

Do not invent statistics or present assumptions as facts. Keep the business realistic, affordable, innovative, and achievable as a short AI prototype.
